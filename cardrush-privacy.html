CardRush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information CardRush collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

Information We Collect

CardRush does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.

The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Game scores and results
  • App settings and preferences
  • Install date (used to determine when to show ads)

Advertising

CardRush uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / IDFA on iOS)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

Ads are requested with the requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly flag set to true, which limits the use of your data for personalized advertising. However, Google may still use device information for frequency capping and fraud prevention.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its advertising services, please visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Advertising ID (Android)

On Android 13 and above, CardRush declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission, which is required by Google Play for apps using AdMob. This permission allows AdMob to access the Android Advertising ID to serve ads and measure their effectiveness. You can reset or opt out of interest-based advertising at any time through your device settings under Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

Data Storage

All game data and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers, do not collect your data, and do not transmit your personal information to any party other than Google AdMob as described above.

Third-Party Services

CardRush integrates the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — advertising network operated by Google LLC.
    Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

CardRush is a general-audience vocabulary learning app and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Your Choices

  • Android: Go to Settings → Google → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • iOS: Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to limit ad tracking across apps.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
